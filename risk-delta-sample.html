What a Risk Delta
looks like

Audit findings record what is wrong. A Risk Delta records what changed after a fix, and says how each figure was produced.

A Risk Delta is the change in an organisation's measured risk position before and after a S.I.N.S. Intervention™.
How deep it goes

Three levels, matched to the evidence

Not every job supports every level. RETACH reports the level the evidence supports and no more.

Level 1

Evidence

Before-and-after facts for the specific fix, such as accounts covered by MFA or backups that passed a restore test.

Shown in this sample
Level 2

Risk Delta

The change in S.I.N.S. pillar scores after a material fix.

Shown in this sample
Level 3

Full CRQ

Modelled expected annual loss in KES before and after, only where the evidence supports a model.

Not shown in this sample
How to read the figures

Every figure says how it was produced

Measured
Directly observed or tested, such as a scan result or a restore test.
Estimated
Calculated from measured data using a defined RETACH method.
Modelled
Produced by the CRQ Engine's actuarial model. Level 3 only.
Assumed
An external or sector-benchmark input, not RETACH's own observation.

Level 3 · Full CRQ

Modelled loss in KES

Not included in this sample. A Full CRQ report adds expected annual loss in KES before and after the interventions, with percentile bands, each labelled Modelled and listing the assumptions used. RETACH does not show a KES figure unless the evidence supports the model.
Limits

What a Risk Delta does not tell you

  • It is not a promise. It reports what changed. It does not say an incident will not happen.
  • These numbers are illustrative. They were written for this page and describe no real organisation.
  • Scores are estimates. Only the Level 1 rows are direct observations.
  • One organisation, before and after. A Risk Delta is not a comparison between organisations or between products.
