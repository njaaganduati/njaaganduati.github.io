RETACH Risk Insights

One public risk signal at a time, linked to a S.I.N.S. pillar, a risk scenario and an intervention, with the modelled effect and where it comes from. Published with the Tuesday Weekly Brief.

A Risk Delta is the change in an organisation's measured risk position before and after a S.I.N.S. Intervention™.

Modelled, not measured. Effects shown here are modelled from published studies, not results RETACH has observed at a client. Measured Risk Delta reads "Not yet measured" until a real engagement produces a before-and-after result, and then only anonymised and with the client's written consent. No money figures appear on this page.

How to read an item

  1. SignalA public source, with its date and link.
  2. Pillar and scenarioWhere it lands in S.I.N.S. and the risk model. RETACH's editorial mapping.
  3. InterventionThe S.I.N.S. Intervention™ that answers it.
  4. Expected effectDirection, plus the published coefficient where one exists.
  5. Measured Risk DeltaReal before-and-after evidence, or "Not yet measured".

About the numbers

Coefficients come from the same table that drives the CRQ Engine, and each carries its source, geography, evidence tier and confidence. The method is aligned with NIST CSF 2.0 and ISO/IEC 27005. The pillar and scenario for each item is an editorial judgement by RETACH, which is why it is labelled. Research is cited, never reproduced. RETACH resells some security products.
