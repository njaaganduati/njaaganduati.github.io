      How the numbers are built
      • Pillar scores from the scan file map to S.I.N.S. sub-scores exactly as the live ad-audit bridge does: Systems = mean of Identity, Credentials and Delegation; Infrastructure = Infrastructure; Network = Detection (proxy); Security = mean of Persistence and GPO Governance.
      • Expected annual loss uses the live CRQ Engine method: Poisson frequency (λKE 0.462 × sector factor × scenario weight × posture and MFA modifier), Lognormal severity, credibility-weighted against the sector band.
      • Fixing a finding is scored with the audit modules' own pillar rule: each open finding costs its pillar Critical 25, High 15, Medium 8 or Low 3 points, raised by 10%, 25% or 50% when it affects 5, 10 or 20+ objects; the pillar score is 100 less those points, never below 0. The projection recomputes the pillar from the findings left open.
      • Default fix cost (internal, tier-5): effort of Critical 8 h, High 4 h, Medium 2 h, Low 1 h at the hourly rate shown. Replace with the scoped quote for each fix; licence or hardware costs go in the same field.
      • Return = annual exposure removed ÷ one-off fix cost. Payback = fix cost ÷ monthly exposure removed. Both are projections, not measured results.
      • B0 → C1 uses each file's own pillar scores, so the measured change reflects the re-scan, not the assumption.
      • Sectors other than SACCO use placeholder benchmark data and are not for client reports.
      • The built-in sample's pillar scores are calculated from its own findings with the module rule, so its projected and measured figures can be compared like for like. Real scan files keep the pillar scores the modules wrote.
